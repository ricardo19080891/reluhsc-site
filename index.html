About

A technology company built for real operations

ReluhSC was founded in South Carolina by Ricardo (Rick) Souza. We build software for the places where work actually happens: the factory floor, the rental house between guests and the family budget at the end of the month.

The name carries a nod to our field. ReLU is the function that lets a neural network keep what matters and drop the noise. We try to build software the same way.
